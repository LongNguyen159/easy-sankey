Easy Sankey User Manual

Enter income and expenses

  1. Open the menu, choose Main, and select a month.
  2. Click Edit input, then Add Entries.
  3. Choose Income, Expense, or Tax. You can add one tax entry per month.
  4. Enter a name and amount. For expenses, you can also choose a category.

Inline calculator

Type a calculation directly into Amount. Both 100 + 50 and 100 50 give 150. Supported operators are "+" and "-".

In Edit input, use Search to find entries or View to group them by cost type, category, or budget.

Track investments and savings

Open the menu, choose Categories, and add or edit a category. Set Classification to Investment contribution, then use that category for outgoing entries such as ETF purchases. Changing the classification also updates past months.

  • Cash surplus is the money left after regular expenses and investment contributions. It includes money in your bank account.
  • Total savings adds investment contributions back to cash surplus. It appears in the monthly overview when you have investment contributions and uses the amount you contributed, excluding changes in market value.
  • Savings rate divides total savings by net income. It shows a dash when net income is zero or negative. Finance Manager uses the totals for the selected period.

The Sankey chart includes investments because they use available cash. Turn on Show expenses only in the distribution chart to see spending by category. Each percentage is a share of total spending.

Use the Sankey chart

On Main, the chart shows where your money came from and where it went. Drag the handle below the chart up or down to change its height. You can also focus the handle and press the Up or Down arrow key.

Use Vertical View or Horizontal View to change the layout. Select Show Trends to compare with the previous month.

Set up recurring income, costs, and investments

Open the menu and choose Monthly Recurring to set up amounts that repeat each month, such as salary, rent, and ETF contributions.

In Edit input, select Apply Monthly Recurring to add them to the selected month. If you change your recurring amounts later, use Update Monthly Recurring in each month you want to refresh.

Set and track budgets

Open the menu and choose Budgets to set limits that repeat each month. On Main, choose Manage budgets to set a limit for the selected month.

To assign an expense, open its actions in Edit input and choose Add to budget. The Budgets section on Main shows how much you have left or how much you went over.

Manage categories

Open the menu and choose Categories. Use Add category or edit an existing category to change its name, icon, and colours.

Review your finances

Open the menu and choose Finance Manager to see income, regular expenses, savings, and cumulative cash surplus over time. Cumulative cash surplus adds up the monthly cash left after expenses and investments in the selected period. Use Show predictions to show or hide future estimates.

Back up your data

Easy Sankey stores your data on your device. Open the menu and choose Settings, then find App data.

Select Export full backup to save a JSON file. Select Import full backup to restore one. Restoring replaces your current app data.

A note from the developer

I built Easy Sankey to keep your financial data on your device. Your entries and budgets are stored in a local SQLite database, and you can use them offline. If you export a backup, you choose where to save it and who to share it with.
